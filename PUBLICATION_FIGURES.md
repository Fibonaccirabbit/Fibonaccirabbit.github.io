# Publication previews

The website displays a short research introduction and a core figure before
any external paper links. English is the default; Chinese descriptions use the
same bibliographic records and image assets.

Only figures are included in the website. Complete source PDFs, author email
addresses, acceptance emails, and review screenshots are not copied into it.
Withheld paper records and figures are kept
outside the website source and public assets, so they are not bundled or served.

MAPF-World's complete figure region is rendered at 600 dpi, preserving the
vector labels layered over its raster artwork. EnvPilot's vector diagram is
rendered at 720 dpi. Other embedded raster figures are extracted at their native
resolution without upscaling. MCGA and H-LIGO retain the resolution actually present in their PDFs;
larger original author artwork would be needed for more detail.

Lossless WebP previews are capped at 2400 pixels wide. Figure dialogs load the
native PNG and provide an original-image link. The HsRu record remains text-only
because its source PDF was not found in the supplied directory.
